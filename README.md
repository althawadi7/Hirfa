## What is Hirfa?

Hirfa (حِرفة) is a bilingual **AR + EN** platform built for makers and buyers across **Bahrain and the GCC**:

| Area | What you get |
|------|----------------|
| **Shops** | Sell handmade products — crochet, pottery, jewelry, candles, sewing, woodwork, arts & more |
| **Courses** | Teach or learn craft skills online |
| **Services / gigs** | Order custom work — design, video, 3D print, crochet-on-demand, and more |
| **App** | Book, browse and buy on iOS & Android |

Live site: [teamhirfa.com](https://teamhirfa.com)

## Who it’s for

- Artisans & home-based makers in Bahrain

- Buyers looking for handmade gifts, candles, books & custom work
